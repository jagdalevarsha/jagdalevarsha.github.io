Hello world.

You just browsed into my biological computer.

If there is one thing I want you to know about me, it's this: I move through the world in awe of its beauty and creativity. Beauty that runs deep. Creativity that makes each moment unfold.

Beauty lives in the totality of any being's being — not in some artistic ideal, but in grounded, ordinary reality. Take the dandelion. So many dimensions on display at once. Its interconnection with soil, sun, wind, and bees. Its ethereal aesthetics. How it reads context cleverly — arriving early spring before other flowers to maximize the chances of seed dispersal, reciprocating with nectar. Its strategic execution: young, it holds the root firmly; old, it surrenders gracefully. Its impact on people — some call it a weed, some make a wish. And how it solves the problem of continuity by leveraging empty space in a parachute and trusting the wind.

These values — of interconnection, systemic thinking, strategic execution, design, and contextual problem solving — are approaches I try to embody in both professional and personal life. The awe inspires the creativity, and the creativity moves me to make things. On the right, you'll find some of what that has produced.

Like the dandelion, I think of this as sending out idea-parachutes into the world, trusting they'll land where they need to. Maybe something transforms — a new thought, a new creation, a new connection.

Now the fun part: it's your turn to make a wish. While we wait for AI to simulate a good breath, let's go old school — use your cursor.

In awe,
Varsha

P.S. This site is dedicated to my first loves — writing and coding. Yes, it's an oxymoron.
