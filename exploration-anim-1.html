Hello world. You just browsed into my biological computer.

I move through the world chasing beauty — in ideas, in objects, in the way light falls at the end of an afternoon. Professionally I've wandered across computer engineering, product design, and user research. Personally, just as many areas.

I'm moved by things that are soft and stubborn at once — delicate enough to scatter in the wind, persistent enough to grow through concrete. That's the kind of work I try to do.

When I'm not lost in the world looking for wonder, wisdom, and wit, I come here to file my inspirations and creations. Hope you find something that lands.

This site is dedicated to my first loves - writing and coding. Yep, it's an oxymoron.
